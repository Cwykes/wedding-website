Our Story

Our story began in 2014, when we first met at a Tae-Kwon-Do class, and started talking after being paired for sparing. From that very first meeting and a clean kick to Jade's head, neither of us could have imagined the beautiful journey that lay ahead. On the 22nd January 2015, our love story officially began.

Sorry Fin!

As the years went on, we shared so many important moments together, including our time at Plantsbrook Sixth Form, growing side by side through some of life’s most memorable years. Although university took us on different paths — with Callum at Nottingham and Jade at Staffordshire — our love only grew stronger. Through every challenge and milestone, we supported one another, both proudly graduating in 2022.

In August 2024, we took another exciting step in our journey and bought our first home, moving in together and setting up a wonderful new chapter filled with love, laughter, and dreams for the future.

Shortly after, October 2024, during a magical and definitely safe road trip around Iceland, our story reached one of its most unforgettable moments. Surrounded by the breathtaking beauty of Gljúfrabúi waterfall, no you didn't pronounce it correctly, Callum asked Jade to spend forever with him — and with a very happy “No no no.....yes,” the next chapter of our love story began.

Now, 2 years later we can’t wait to celebrate our special day with you, the people we love most, and begin our forever together.

Venue

Hanbury Barns

We are excited to host our wedding at Hanbury Barns, a romantic countryside haven in the heart of a National Forest. Just a short drive from Burton upon Trent, Hanbury Barns blends rustic charm with soft, modern elegance. Restored red‑brick barns, peaceful lakeside views, and warm natural light create a setting that feels intimate, relaxed, and beautifully timeless.

FAQ

Here are a few frequently asked questions:

When should I RSVP by?

We kindly ask that you RSVP no later than 10th August 2026. If you do not RSVP by this date, we will assume you are unable to attend.

Can I bring a guest?

No, we regretfully ask that you do not bring a guest unless specifically invited.

What is the dress code?

Just general wedding dress code innit, so feel free to dress accordingly or not but you will be shamed for being unprepared.

Are children welcome?

If children are invited their names will be listed on the invitation.

Is there parking available?

Yes, there is parking available on site at the venue. Cars can be left overnight to be driven off by 10am the following morning.

Is the venue cashless?

Yes, the venue is cashless. All transactions must be made by card.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony will begin at 1:30pm, so please make sure you arrive at the venue no later than 1:10pm. The earliest arrival time is 12:30pm.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive at the venue at 6:30pm. We will begin with the first dance at 7:00pm.
